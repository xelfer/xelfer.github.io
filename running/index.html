Race Day Strategy

Start-10km Get behind the 4:45 flag and stay there, never in front of it. Adrenaline, a downhill bridge and 20,000 people will all pull you forward, and this is the only part of the race where discipline changes the outcome. If you lose the group here you will not get them back. Expect the splits to jump around — even effort means about 7:00 up the climbs and 6:20 on the descents, and a slow split on a hill is the plan working.
10-21km Hold position and feed. Nothing else to do. Halfway is around 2 hours 22. Stop reading the pace field on your watch — with even effort it will say alarming things all day. Follow the flag and let the pacer do the arithmetic. If it feels easy here, that is exactly what it is meant to feel like. Do not spend it.
21-32km The hills are here and the descents are the real risk — even effort means genuinely quicker running downhill, and drifting is your documented failure mode. Stay level with the flag. This is the stretch where staying boring pays for the last ten kilometres.
32-42km This is the decision, and the only one you make all day. Beyond here is further than you have ever run. If you feel strong, leave the group and press — six twenty a kilometre from here turns four forty five into four forty. If you do not feel strong, hold what you have and let them go. Both are good races. If you are struggling, take the five hours — that is still a good first marathon and it beats walking from thirty five. Walking is not failing.
Goal 4:45, which is 6:45 per kilometre. Sit with the flag and it takes care of itself.
HR cap Under 168 until 30km, then let it climb. This is the governor. The 30km ran at 158 average, so 6:45 pace will sit in the low to mid 160s; if it is over 168 before 30km the pace gives, not the cap.
Fuel Ten feeds. A Maurten before the start, then every 30 minutes — Maurten, squeeze, caffeine, repeating. About three hundred grams on course, which is sixty three grams an hour. Three caffeine gels only, at ninety minutes, three hours and four thirty. The schedule is a default, not a contract — if your stomach complains, skip or halve the next feed, take water, and reassess at the one after. Drop the caffeine first.
Aid stations Water every 2-3km, Powerade at every 5km marker. Walk through for water — five seconds at 6:45/km costs nothing and beats inhaling it. Portaloos at every drink station.
